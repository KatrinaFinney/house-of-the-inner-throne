# Founding Content Runway

This runway introduces Shrine of the Inner Throne before product promotion begins. Each core idea can become a carousel, faceless short video, voiceover, Story sequence, email paragraph, and manuscript quotation.

## Content goals

1. Establish the new Shrine name.
2. Explain the Inner Throne in plain language.
3. Build trust through useful teachings.
4. Establish privacy, protection, and cultural-care boundaries.
5. Introduce the Archive before introducing products.
6. Prepare the audience to understand Ancestor Money as disciplined practice rather than novelty.

## Four-week runway

| Week | Theme | Primary content | Supporting content | Invitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Enter the Shrine | A sacred space for the throne within | What the Inner Throne means; meet the Three Pillars | Enter the Shrine and begin with the Archive |
| 2 | Protection before access | Privacy is a form of protection | Sacred boundaries; why every practice should not be posted | Read a Protection manuscript |
| 3 | Power through discipline | Sovereignty is a practice | Attention is spiritual currency; tools do not replace discipline | Enter the Power pillar |
| 4 | Prosperity in right relationship | Prosperity must be held by structure | Offering, circulation, stewardship; introduction to ancestral currency | Join the Ancestor Money waitlist |

Shrine of the Inner Throne is a sacred digital shrine devoted to spiritual sovereignty, ancestral remembrance, and the disciplined cultivation of Protection, Power, and Prosperity.

The Shrine will hold the Inner Throne Manuscripts, ritual foundations, and carefully prepared tools for sacred work. It will also preserve an essential boundary: everything sacred does not need to be displayed.

This is a place for study, practice, remembrance, and return.

Enter the Shrine. The work begins within.

## Founding post 02: What is the Inner Throne?

**Hook:** The Inner Throne is the seat no one else can occupy for you.

**Teaching:** It is your capacity to discern, choose, practice, and remain spiritually responsible without surrendering your authority to fear, spectacle, or another person.

**Invitation:** Begin with the first manuscript, “Spiritual Sovereignty.”

## Founding post 03: The Three Pillars

**Hook:** A throne cannot remain steady without structure.

- Protection preserves your freedom.
- Power restores your authority.
- Prosperity makes blessing visible and usable.

**Invitation:** Which pillar is asking for your attention now?

## Founding post 04: Privacy is protection

**Hook:** Sacred does not mean public.

Teach that spiritual privacy can protect attention, intention, cultural integrity, and the practitioner's autonomy. Do not show a private altar as the visual.

## Founding post 05: Protection before prosperity

**Hook:** What arrives must have somewhere safe to remain.

Connect boundaries, habits, stewardship, and emotional capacity to sustainable prosperity. Position protection as the structure that allows increase to be held.

## Sustainable weekly rhythm

| Day | Role |
|---|---|
| Monday | Manuscript teaching |
| Wednesday | Atmospheric faceless short video |
| Friday | Pillar or ritual-foundation education |
| Sunday | Reflection, invocation, or email |

Four strong weekly contributions are enough for the founding phase. One core teaching should be repackaged across formats instead of creating unrelated material for every platform.
