# Week 1 Launch Kit: Enter the Shrine

## Purpose

Introduce Shrine of the Inner Throne as a complete sacred identity. The audience should understand what the Shrine is, what the Inner Throne means, and how to enter through the Archive and the Three Pillars.

## Core message

**Enter the Shrine. The work begins within.**

Shrine of the Inner Throne is a sacred digital space for spiritual sovereignty, ancestral remembrance, ritual intelligence, and disciplined prosperity.

## Primary caption

Enter the Shrine.

Shrine of the Inner Throne is a sacred digital space devoted to spiritual sovereignty, ancestral remembrance, ritual intelligence, and the disciplined cultivation of Protection, Power, and Prosperity.

The Inner Throne is the seat of authority within the self. It is the place from which we discern, choose, practice, remember, and remain responsible for the power we cultivate.

Within the Shrine, the Inner Throne Manuscripts preserve 44 teachings across four volumes. Each manuscript returns the practitioner to discipline, responsibility, and the sacred work of governing the self.

The Archive is open. The Storehouse will follow in its appointed time.

Enter the Shrine. The work begins within.

## Faceless short video

### Format

- Vertical 9:16
- 20–25 seconds
- Slow, restrained movement
- Dark marble, parchment, ember, smoke, and gold light
- No private altar imagery

### Shot list and on-screen copy

| Time | Visual | On-screen copy |
|---:|---|---|
| 0–3 sec | Closed marbled doors in low light | Enter the Shrine. |
| 3–7 sec | Gold light appearing beneath the doors | A sacred space for the throne within. |
| 7–12 sec | Closed manuscript, parchment, or turning page | Spiritual sovereignty. Ancestral remembrance. Ritual intelligence. |
| 12–17 sec | Three restrained gold lights or three architectural details | Protection. Power. Prosperity. |
| 17–22 sec | Doors opening into light, without revealing an altar | The Archive is open. |
| 22–25 sec | Wordmark on dark background | The work begins within. |

### Voiceover

“Enter the Shrine. A sacred digital space for the throne within. Here, spiritual sovereignty is studied through ancestral remembrance, ritual intelligence, and disciplined prosperity. Protection. Power. Prosperity. The Archive is open. The work begins within.”

### Accessibility caption

A sequence of dark architectural imagery, parchment, smoke, and gold light introduces Shrine of the Inner Throne and its three pillars: Protection, Power, and Prosperity.

## Platform packaging

### Instagram and TikTok

Use the vertical video as the primary post. Keep the caption’s first line visible before the fold:

**Enter the Shrine.**

Close with:

**Begin with “Spiritual Sovereignty,” the first Inner Throne Manuscript.**

### LinkedIn

Use the seven-slide carousel. Lead with the purpose rather than presenting it as a product announcement:

“I built Shrine of the Inner Throne as a digital archive for spiritual sovereignty, ancestral remembrance, and disciplined prosperity. Its 44 manuscripts are organized around a simple conviction: spiritual authority must be cultivated within.”

Close with a link to the first manuscript.

### YouTube Shorts

Use the same vertical video with the title:

**Enter the Shrine | The Work Begins Within**

Description:

“Shrine of the Inner Throne is a sacred digital archive organized around Protection, Power, and Prosperity. Begin with the first manuscript, Spiritual Sovereignty.”
