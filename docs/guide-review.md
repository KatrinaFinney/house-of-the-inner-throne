## Latest revisions

- Elemental Magic now opens with the qualities and relationships of the elements: water's depth and adaptability, earth's roots and endurance, fire's activation and transformation, and air as spoken prayer, affirmation, and words of power.
- Added elemental combinations and the difference between a quick beginning and sustained care. Physical processes are distinguished from spiritual symbolism.
- Candles now covers birthday/small candles for brief workings, larger candles for sustained sequences, practical examples, and candle safety. Burn time is not presented as a guaranteed manifestation time.
- Added Astrology & Ritual Timing: eight lunar phases, all 12 Sun-sign seasons, seven planetary weekdays, transits, and practical examples. Verify actual dates and local times before using any dated timing.
- Added The Opposite Mirror, subtitled Shadow Work Through Zodiac Opposites. Includes all six zodiac pairs in both directions. The specific shadow interpretation and examples are identified as the Shrine's framework, rather than a universal astrological rule or diagnosis.
